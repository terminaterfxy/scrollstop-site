Scroll Brake（回神）隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-16

简体中文

Scroll Brake（回神）重视你的隐私。简单来说：我们不收集你的数据，所有规则和统计都保留在你的设备上。

我们收集什么

Scroll Brake 不收集个人信息，没有账号系统，不运行开发者服务器，也不包含广告、分析或追踪 SDK。App 不会将使用数据上传到开发者或第三方。

Screen Time 权限

Scroll Brake 使用苹果的 Family Controls、Device Activity 和 Managed Settings 框架：

  • 你亲自选择要监控的 App 或分类。
  • iOS 在设备上计算这些所选对象的使用时长。
  • 达到你设置的时间后，如果你选的是强制休息，iOS 显示系统遮罩；选的是仅提醒，则只发通知，不遮罩。
  • 首页显示守护期间回神自己记录到的用时：普通监控的 App 记整个 App 的使用时间，开启「只监控短视频」的 App 记短视频估算。暂停不计用时。不读取系统屏幕使用时间报告，记录可能延迟。

Scroll Brake 看不到或读取不到聊天内容、照片、键盘输入、其他 App 的通知内容、网页内容或第三方 App 内正在显示的内容。开发者无法得知你选择了哪些 App，也无法查看你的使用时长。

通知权限

通知用于本轮额度即将用完、仅提醒模式到点及自动恢复完成的提示。每日自动开启不依赖通知权限，系统通话复查不发送普通预警。普通模式拒绝通知权限不会影响计时和到点遮罩。短视频模式选择强制休息时，通知可用则在确认本轮提醒送达后复核；通知不可用则不提前提醒，独立复核通过后仍会开始休息。强制休息暂时遮住整个 App，聊天也会暂停。仅查询回神自身通知的送达记录，不读取其他 App 的通知。

设备上的数据

以下内容只保存在这台设备上：

  • 你选择的 App 或分类 token；
  • 每个对象的提醒时间；
  • 休息时长；
  • 提醒计划状态；
  • 本地提醒、休息统计和有限的诊断日志。
  • 短视频监控的原始分段回调记录（保留 3 个统计日）及每对象每日的视频、非视频、未能判断秒数（保留 90 个统计日）；
  • 识别体验进度、结果及是否已关闭。

这些数据不会上传、出售或分享。卸载 App 或由系统清除 App 数据后，本地数据可能被删除。

通话保护

开启通话保护时，App 只查询系统是否报告正在通话，不读取通话内容、麦克风或音频路由。没有报告不代表没有微信通话；可按需要提前暂停提醒。

短视频监控

某个 App 的「只监控短视频」开着时使用此模式，可在单项设置里开关；关着时按整个 App 的使用时间计时。此模式仅在本机读取整机物理网络接口的收发字节计数，以估算疑似视频时间；不读取网络通信内容、域名、聊天内容或微信通话记录。整机计数不能准确归属到所选 App，可能误判或漏计。

最近一次接口计数、采样时刻、视频估算累计、回神自身提醒的送达状态和有限诊断日志保存在设备本地，不上传。无法判断的采样不会当成非视频。关闭模式取消当前监控轮次。

历史测试记录

1.3（41）已移除手动流量测量工具。此前保存的场景、网络、测量基线及结果仍仅留在本机；更新不会上传或自动删除这些历史数据，当前版本不再提供测量与复制入口。

其他数据

Scroll Brake 不读取提醒事项、日历、健康、联系人、位置、照片或麦克风数据。

English

Scroll Brake respects your privacy. In short: we do not collect your data, and all rules and statistics stay on your device.

What we collect

Scroll Brake collects no personal information. It has no account system, no developer-operated server, and no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app does not upload usage data to the developer or any third party.

Screen Time access

Scroll Brake uses Apple's Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks:

  • You choose the apps or categories to monitor.
  • iOS calculates on-device usage duration for those selected items.
  • When a configured limit is reached, iOS shows a system shield if you chose "Require a break". If you chose "Notify only", you get a notification and no shield.
  • The home screen shows time Scroll Brake itself recorded while protection is on: whole-app usage for normally monitored apps, and the short-video estimate for apps with "Monitor short videos only" turned on. Pauses do not count. The app does not read Screen Time reports, and records may be delayed.

Scroll Brake cannot see or read chats, photos, keyboard input, other apps’ notification content, webpages, or content displayed inside another app. The developer cannot tell which apps you selected or see your usage duration.

Notification access

Notifications warn before a round reaches its allowance, notify at the limit in notification-only mode, and may report that automatic protection has resumed. Automatic restart is independent of notification permission. System-call rechecks do not send ordinary warnings. In ordinary mode, denying notifications does not affect measurement or shields. In short-video mode with required breaks, an available notification is followed by delivery verification and an independent review. If notifications are unavailable, the same independent review can start a break without advance warning. A required break temporarily covers the entire app, including messaging. Only the app's own delivery records are checked.

Data stored on your device

The following stays only on this device:

  • Tokens representing the apps or categories you select;
  • The limit for each selected item;
  • Your break duration;
  • Whether the reminder plan is enabled;
  • Local reminder, break, and limited diagnostic logs.
  • Raw short-video monitoring observations for three statistical days, and per-item daily video, non-video and undetermined totals for ninety statistical days;
  • Detection tryout progress, results and dismissal state.

This data is never uploaded, sold, or shared. It may be deleted when you uninstall the app or the system removes the app's data.

Call protection

When enabled, call protection only checks whether iOS reports an active call. It does not read call content, microphone or audio routes. A missing report does not rule out a WeChat call; pause reminders when needed.

Short-video monitoring

This mode is used while "Monitor short videos only" is on for an app; you can switch it in the app's settings. When it is off, the whole app's usage is timed. It reads device-wide physical interface byte counters locally to estimate video-like usage. It does not read network payloads, domains, chats or WeChat call records. Device-wide counters cannot accurately attribute traffic to the selected app, so estimates can miss or misclassify usage.

The latest interface counters, sample timestamps, estimated usage totals, the app's own warning delivery state, and limited diagnostic logs stay on the device and are not uploaded. An undetermined sample is not treated as non-video. Turning the mode off cancels the current monitoring round.

Historical test records

Version 1.3 (41) removes the manual traffic measurement tool. Previously saved activity and network labels, baselines and results remain on the device. Updating does not upload or automatically delete these records. The current version no longer offers measurement or copy controls.

Other data

Scroll Brake does not access Reminders, Calendar, Health, Contacts, Location, Photos, or Microphone data.
